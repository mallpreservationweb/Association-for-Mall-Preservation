Get Involved

Ways to Get Involved

There are many ways to support AMP, from donating and becoming a partner to starting a chapter and joining national initiatives. This page highlights the opportunities available to help preserve and activate mall spaces in your community.

Partner with AMP

Host events, open space for nonprofit activations, and support local entrepreneurship through adaptive reuse.

Launch a Chapter

Start a student or youth-led chapter to organize community activities, research reuse strategies, and mobilize local volunteers.

Support Events

Donate time, expertise, or sponsorship to power pop-ups, workshops, and civic programs in mall spaces.

Donate Today

Your support helps fund chapter growth, events, research, and community programming that keep malls active and connected.

Contributions help AMP expand outreach, host activations, and support student-led initiatives in underused mall spaces.

Contact About Donations

Become a National Member

National membership is a great opportunity to stay involved with AMP, and can even be done without joining a specific chapter. It is a way to support the organization, connect with a wider network, and participate in national events and initiatives.

Benefits include: access to national events, member updates, networking opportunities, and invitations to volunteer and leadership initiatives.
