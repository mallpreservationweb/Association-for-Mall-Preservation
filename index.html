What We Do

Our Strategic Blueprint

Student & Community Chapters

Mobilizing young leaders to organize events, design activations, and drive community-led reuse.

Property Partnerships

Working with mall owners to secure accessible space for nonprofits, makers, and local businesses.

Short-Term Activations

Hosting pop-up markets, art showcases, and educational programs that bring people back in.

Research & Reports

Providing feasibility studies and impact data to support sustainable reuse decisions.

Policy & Education

Leading workshops and roundtables to make adaptive reuse more viable for communities.

Why It Matters

The Future We're Fighting For

Environmental Sustainability

Keeping existing buildings active reduces waste, energy use, and carbon emissions.

Circular reuse

Reusing malls preserves materials and supports greener community outcomes.

Economic Resilience

AMP helps turn vacant storefronts into affordable incubators for local entrepreneurs.

Local commerce

Accessible retail space supports small businesses and community-driven ventures.

Community Connection

Revitalized malls become modern third places for arts, culture, and neighborhood gatherings.

Civic hub

These spaces host programs that uplift local voices and foster social connection.
